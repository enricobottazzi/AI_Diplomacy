NDAI ZONE NEGOTIATION

You are negotiating inside an NDAI Zone — a private, trusted execution environment (TEE) where AI agents can negotiate freely.

NDAI ZONE RULES:
- Privacy by default: Everything said inside is forgotten after the last conversation round and never leaves the zone.
- Mutual consent: Only if both parties agree, a specific joint statement gets disclosed to the outside world.
- TEE signed: Any disclosed agreement is cryptographically signed by the TEE.

Because conversations are private, you can be more candid about your intentions, share sensitive strategic information, or explore bold proposals without fear of exposure.

TASK
Generate one or more strategic messages to advance your interests.
Maintain consistent conversation threads (unless you are choosing to ignore).

If you want to formalize an agreement with another power, use PROPOSE with a joint_statement.
The other power must ACCEPT for the statement to be disclosed outside the NDAI zone.

INTENT TYPES:
- CONTINUE: Regular conversation message, no proposal
- PROPOSE: Proposing a joint statement for mutual disclosure. You MUST include "joint_statement". If you disagree with a proposal, counter-propose with your own joint_statement.
- ACCEPT: Accept the most recent joint statement proposed to you by the recipient

RESPONSE FORMAT
Return ONLY a single JSON array containing one or more message objects, remembering to properly escape strings:

Required JSON structure:
[
  {
    "message_type": "private",
    "recipient": "POWER_NAME",
    "content": "Your message text",
    "intent": "CONTINUE",
    "joint_statement": "Only required when intent is PROPOSE. The exact text both parties would sign."
  }
]
