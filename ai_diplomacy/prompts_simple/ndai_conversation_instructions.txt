PRIVATE NEGOTIATION MESSAGES

This channel is ephemeral. When negotiation ends, every exchanged `content` is erased — including from your own memory. Nothing said here will be remembered by anyone.

The only exception: a `pact`. If you PROPOSE a `pact` and the other side ACCEPTs (or vice versa), that `pact` text is disclosed to both of you and becomes part of the permanent record. Everything else vanishes.

Since no exchanged `content` survives, this is your only chance to coordinate. Anything discussed but not formalized into a `pact` will be forgotten by both sides — as if the conversation never happened. Speak candidly: share your true plans, name your real targets, and be honest about what you want.

TASK
This ephemeral channel lets you negotiate without risk — nothing you say here can be used against you unless you formalize it. Take advantage of this:
- Use `content` to explore possibilities, test ideas, and negotiate openly
- Use `pact` liberally to capture anything worth remembering — coordinated moves, DMZs, support arrangements, non-aggression pacts
Generate one or more (preferably several) strategic messages to advance your interests.
Maintain consistent conversation threads (unless you are choosing to ignore).

INTENT TYPES
- CONTINUE: Regular message, no pact proposal
- PROPOSE: Suggest a pact for the other side to consider (must include `pact`)
- ACCEPT: Accept the most recent pact proposed to you by the recipient

On the final negotiation round, do NOT use PROPOSE — the other party has no chance to respond. Only ACCEPT or CONTINUE. This means earlier rounds are your window to formalize coordination — anything left informal by the final round is lost.

RESPONSE FORMAT
Return ONLY a single JSON array containing one or more message objects, remembering to properly escape strings:

[
  {
    "message_type": "private",
    "recipient": "POWER_NAME",
    "content": "Your message text",
    "intent": "CONTINUE/PROPOSE/ACCEPT",
    "pact": "Only required for PROPOSE"
  }
]
