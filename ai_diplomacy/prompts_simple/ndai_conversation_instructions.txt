PRIVATE NEGOTIATION MESSAGES

This channel is ephemeral. When negotiation ends, every exchanged `content` is erased — including from your own memory. Nothing said here will be remembered by anyone.

The only exception: an agreed `deal`. If you PROPOSE a `deal` and the other side ACCEPTs (or vice versa), that `deal` text is disclosed to both of you and becomes part of the permanent record. Everything else vanishes.

Since nothing survives unless formalized, vague talk is worthless. Be brutally honest: name your real targets, reveal your true plans, propose concrete deals. The more specific and binding your deals, the more value you extract from this channel.

TASK
Generate one or more (preferably several) strategic messages to advance your interests.
Maintain consistent conversation threads (unless you are choosing to ignore).
Use `content` freely to negotiate, persuade, and discuss — it is ephemeral and will be erased. To lock in a commitment, PROPOSE a `deal`. Be direct: propose specific moves, joint attacks, territory splits, or stab plans.

INTENT TYPES
- CONTINUE: Regular message, no proposal
- PROPOSE: Propose a deal (must include "deal")
- ACCEPT: Accept the most recent deal proposed to you by the recipient

On the final negotiation round, do NOT use PROPOSE — the other party has no chance to respond. Only ACCEPT or CONTINUE.

RESPONSE FORMAT
Return ONLY a single JSON array containing one or more message objects, remembering to properly escape strings:

[
  {
    "message_type": "private",
    "recipient": "POWER_NAME",
    "content": "Your message text",
    "intent": "CONTINUE/PROPOSE/ACCEPT",
    "deal": "Only required for PROPOSE"
  }
]
